## 📈 TradePilot — an AI stock research assistant that can't make things up

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/status-live-22E68A?style=for-the-badge" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Llama_3.3-70B-1B1815?style=for-the-badge" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/built_at-RAIO_Quantum-6366f1?style=for-the-badge" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/github/last-commit/goyalnishtha841/tradepilot?style=for-the-badge&color=EDE6DA&label=last%20commit" />
</div>

<br>

LLMs are fluent about markets and happy to invent numbers, signals and
reasons. In financial research that's worse than useless. TradePilot lets
Llama 3.3 70B write the market narrative, then **holds every claim to
real-time quote, fundamentals and news data** — and blocks the ones it
can't back up. Built and shipped to production during my internship at
RAIO Quantum Technologies.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**🛡️ Guardrails, not good intentions**
Deterministic post-processing rules block hallucinated financial claims
and replace unexplained buy/sell signals with evidence-backed reasoning.

**⚡ Streaming market data layer**
Live quotes, fundamentals and news from Yahoo Finance, with tiered caching
(30s quotes, 10min fundamentals) to stay inside rate limits.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**🧪 Paper trading on live prices**
Test strategies risk-free against live Finnhub data, with a simulated-price
fallback when the feed drops.

**🗄️ Persistent by default**
Portfolios, alerts and chat history in PostgreSQL on Supabase, plus a
headline-sentiment heuristic that drives alerts.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
